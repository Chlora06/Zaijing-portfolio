About

Hi, I’m {{ name }}.

I’m a UX researcher who turns ambiguity into decisions teams can act on. For the past decade I’ve embedded with product, design, and engineering teams across fintech, healthcare, emerging markets, and consumer hardware.

My work ranges from quick usability checks to multi-week field studies — but it always points the same direction: research that travels. Findings that don’t sit in a deck, but change what gets built. That means tight questions, honest evidence, and recommendations prioritized by impact, not volume.

Evidence over opinion
