About

Hi, I’m {{ name }}.

I’m a UX designer and researcher passionate about creating meaningful, human-centered experiences. My background spans Computer Science and Visual Communication Design, now deepened by graduate study in Human Factors Ergonomics at SJSU.

I’m particularly drawn to equity-focused design — I believe every product should be accessible and inclusive from the first sketch. My work ranges from simulator experiments studying driver behavior, to high-fidelity Figma prototypes for consumer apps. Every project starts with the same question: what do users actually need, and what’s standing in their way?

How I Work
01

Equity first

Inclusive, accessible design isn’t a checklist — it’s a commitment built into every wireframe and prototype from the start.

02

Evidence over assumption

I reach for research tools before design tools. A well-designed study surfaces the real problem before any pixels are pushed.

03

Iterate relentlessly

Design gets better through testing. Every prototype is a hypothesis; every user session is data that sharpens the next version.
